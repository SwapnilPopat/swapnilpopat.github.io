Architecture Notes

Patterns, Principles & Technical Decision Notes

Practical architecture thinking for industrial IoT, smart factory platforms, enterprise integrations, digital twins, DevOps, and AI-enabled manufacturing systems.

Principle

Design for Loose Coupling

Avoid hidden dependencies between platform services, UI layers, integration logic, and external systems. Keep boundaries visible and intentional.

Key Trade-offs & Guardrails

Primary Driver: Prevent changes in shop-floor edge devices from breaking analytics or MES consumers.

Pattern: Asynchronous message queues (RabbitMQ/MQTT) with strict canonical data models.

Guardrail: Avoid synchronous REST calls on industrial telemetry ingest paths.

Pattern

Digital Twin Modeling

Model assets, machines, lines, plants, and products using reusable templates and clear inheritance rules. Keep behavior and data ownership explicit.

Key Trade-offs & Guardrails

Primary Driver: Standardization of plant equipment across global manufacturing lines.

Pattern: ThingWorx ThingTemplates & ThingShapes with clean property inheritance.

Guardrail: Keep business logic decoupled from physical hardware addressing.

Integration

MES ↔ ERP ↔ PLM Integration

Treat integration as a product capability, not as one-time plumbing. Define ownership, event contracts, error handling, and observability from day one.

Key Trade-offs & Guardrails

Primary Driver: End-to-end traceability from engineering design (PLM) to plant execution (MES) and financials (ERP).

Pattern: Event-driven architecture with SAP API Management and dead-letter queue resilience.

Guardrail: Enforce idempotency on every inbound production transaction.

Standard

ISA-95 Thinking

Use ISA-95 to clarify responsibilities across enterprise, operations, control, and physical process layers.

Key Trade-offs & Guardrails

Primary Driver: Clarify boundaries between Level 0-2 (PLC/SCADA), Level 3 (MES/MOM), and Level 4 (ERP/Business).

Pattern: Purdue Enterprise Reference Architecture mapping with Kepware industrial connectivity.

Guardrail: Do not bypass Level 3 for control-plane decisions directly to the cloud.

DevOps

Observable IIoT Platforms

Industrial platforms need structured logs, health checks, metrics, dashboards, alerting, and incident playbooks.

Key Trade-offs & Guardrails

Primary Driver: Rapid root-cause analysis across thousands of distributed edge connections.

Pattern: Splunk, Prometheus, and Grafana unified telemetry with custom synthetic health probes.

Guardrail: Establish telemetry drop alerts before edge buffers overflow.

AI

AI in Manufacturing Architecture

AI should be introduced where data quality, feedback loops, business ownership, and measurable value are clearly defined.

Key Trade-offs & Guardrails

Primary Driver: Closed-loop predictive quality, anomaly detection, and automated root-cause guidance.

Pattern: Edge-cloud hybrid inferencing: low-latency anomaly checks at edge, deep LLM/analytical reasoning in cloud.

Guardrail: Human-in-the-loop validation for all physical parameter adjustments.
