Architecture Notes

Patterns, Principles & Technical Decision Notes

Practical architecture thinking for industrial IoT, smart factory platforms, enterprise integrations, digital twins, DevOps, and AI-enabled manufacturing systems.

Principle

Design for Loose Coupling

Avoid hidden dependencies between platform services, UI layers, integration logic, and external systems. Keep boundaries visible and intentional.

Pattern

Digital Twin Modeling

Model assets, machines, lines, plants, and products using reusable templates and clear inheritance rules. Keep behavior and data ownership explicit.

Integration

MES ↔ ERP ↔ PLM Integration

Treat integration as a product capability, not as one-time plumbing. Define ownership, event contracts, error handling, and observability from day one.

Standard

ISA-95 Thinking

Use ISA-95 to clarify responsibilities across enterprise, operations, control, and physical process layers.

DevOps

Observable IIoT Platforms

Industrial platforms need structured logs, health checks, metrics, dashboards, alerting, and incident playbooks.

AI

AI in Manufacturing Architecture

AI should be introduced where data quality, feedback loops, business ownership, and measurable value are clearly defined.
